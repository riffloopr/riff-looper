## Detailed description (paste into the "Description" field)

Learning guitar from YouTube videos? Riff Looper turns any video into a practice tool. Loop the tricky bits, slow them down without changing the pitch, and control everything with your foot so both hands stay on the guitar.

🎸 LOOPING
• Set the start and end of a passage with [ A and B ]. The loop starts immediately.
• Drag the loop boundaries on a dedicated timeline. With zoom, you can set them to a tenth of a second, even in long videos.
• Fine-tune in 0.1 s steps (Shift: 1 s)
• Save and name loops per video, e.g. "Intro lick" or "Solo bars 5–8". They're back the next time you open the video.

🐢 TEMPO
• 25–200 % without pitch change, in 5 % steps or via the 50/60/70/75/80/90/100 % presets
• Speed trainer: start slow and let the tempo rise automatically, e.g. +5 % every 3 repetitions, until you reach your target speed.
• Optional pause between repetitions to breathe or reset your hand position

🦶 MIDI FOOT SWITCH – HANDS FREE
• Works with any USB MIDI foot switch or controller (Control Change, Note, Program Change)
• MIDI learn: click a function, press the switch, done
• Smart button for one-switch setups: 1st press sets A, 2nd press sets B and starts the loop, 3rd press stops it.
• An expression pedal controls the tempo continuously.
• Supports momentary and latching switches
• "A pre-roll" compensates for your reaction time so the first note isn't cut off.

⌨️ KEYBOARD
Alt (Mac: ⌥) + A / B / L / S / J / ↑ / ↓ and more

✨ ALSO
• The panel sits right inside the player. You can move or collapse it, and it works in full screen.
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. All settings stay in your browser.
• Open source (MIT license)

Note: The panel is currently in German; an English interface is planned.

MIDI ACCESS
For MIDI control, Chrome asks once whether youtube.com may access MIDI devices. The extension only reads button presses from your controller. It sends nothing to the device or the internet.
